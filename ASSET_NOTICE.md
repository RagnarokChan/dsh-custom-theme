`assets/` 保留项目原有的 16 份素材。原始素材字节不因本次主题迁移而改变。

`lib/assets.json` 记录每个文件的长度和 SHA256，构建和测试用于检测遗漏或变更。`lib/settings-edge-map.svg` 由构建脚本组合原图，非新生成的插画。

个人自定义背景保存在使用者自己的 DSH 插件配置中，不属于仓库内容，也不应提交到 Git。

The 16 files in `assets/` are preserved byte-for-byte from the original project. `lib/assets.json` records their sizes and SHA256 hashes. The generated `lib/settings-edge-map.svg` composes existing artwork; it is not a newly generated illustration.

Personal backgrounds are stored only in each user's plugin configuration. They are not repository content and must not be committed to Git.
